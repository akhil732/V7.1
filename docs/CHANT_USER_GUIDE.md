# Vāgdhenu Sanskrit Chant Studio — User Guide

Welcome to the **Vāgdhenu Sanskrit Chant Studio** within Jyothishya Sanathanam!

---

## 🌟 Features

1. **Auto Chandas Meter Detection**
   - Simply paste any Sanskrit shloka (e.g., from the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, or Stotras).
   - The studio detects whether it is **Anuṣṭubh** (8 syllables/quarter), **Upajāti** (11 syllables), **Vasantatilakā** (14 syllables), **Śikhariṇī** (17 syllables), or **Śārdūlavikrīḍita** (19 syllables).

2. **Audio Synthesis & Player**
   - Press **Render Chant** to generate clear 24kHz traditional Vedic pārāyaṇa audio.
   - Play/Pause, seek along the waveform, and view real-time elapsed time.

3. **Audio Download**
   - Save the synthesized `.wav` chanting recording directly to your device.

4. **Curated Presets**
   - **Gāyatrī Mantra** (Rigveda 3.62.10)
   - **Bhagavad Gita 2.47** (Karma Yoga shloka)
   - **Ganesha Vandana** (Vakratunda Mahakaya)
   - **Mahamrityunjaya Mantra** (Rudra healing mantra)
   - **Shanti Mantra** (Sarve Bhavantu Sukhinah)

5. **Voice Style Seed Control**
   - Adjust the **Prosody Seed (1 - 100)** to explore different reciter cadences and pitch characteristics (Seed 60 is standard male recitation).

---

## 📖 How to Use

### On the Home Dashboard:
- The **Vāgdhenu Sanskrit Chant** widget appears right on your home screen.
- Select a preset or type a verse, and click **Render Chant** for instant listening.
- Click **Open Chant Studio** to access the full-screen studio.

### In the Chant Studio:
1. Navigate via the **Chant** tab on the bottom navigation bar.
2. Choose from curated presets or paste your own Devanagari / IAST verse.
3. Select your desired meter (or keep **AUTO** for automatic prosody analysis).
4. Click **Render Chant (24kHz)**.
5. Listen with full audio player controls, scrub the timeline, or download your audio.
